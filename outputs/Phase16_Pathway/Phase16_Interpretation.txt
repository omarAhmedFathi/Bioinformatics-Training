Phase 16: Metabolite Functional and Pathway Discovery
=====================================================

METHODOLOGY
-----------
1. ID Mapping: Mapped internal dataset names to standard HMDB and KEGG IDs.
2. Chemical Taxonomy: Assigned superclass and class to each metabolite.
3. Pathway Annotation: Retrieved biological pathways from KEGG.
4. Enrichment Analysis: Hypergeometric test to identify over-represented
   pathways among the significant metabolites from prior phases.

RESULTS & INTERPRETATION
------------------------
No pathways reached nominal significance (p < 0.05), potentially due to small panel size.

Literature Context:
Many of the key classes in this panel (BCAAs, Acylcarnitines, Aromatic AAs)
are robust biomarkers of insulin resistance and incident Type 2 Diabetes.
Elevated BCAAs and short-chain acylcarnitines reflect altered mitochondrial
catabolism in obesity.
